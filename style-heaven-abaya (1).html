About Style Heaven

Modest fashion. Timeless elegance.

Style Heaven Abaya is a boutique abaya store in Kurla West built around one idea: modest dressing should never feel like a compromise. Every piece is chosen for its fabric, cut and finish first, so what you wear feels considered rather than off the rack.

From everyday essentials to occasion-ready luxury pieces, the collection is built for women who want their abaya to feel as intentional as the rest of their wardrobe.
